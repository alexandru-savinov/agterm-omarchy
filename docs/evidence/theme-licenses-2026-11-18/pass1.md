## Judgment calls

**Sources and policy**
- **Sources.** No theme file at this commit has a header comment; all 819 contain only `key = value` lines. The credits in `CREDITS.md` and `README-CREDITS.md` are the only source.
- **Name matching.**
  - Credit subjects were split into names and matched exactly after normalising case and punctuation. The specific line wins over a family line (Tomorrow Night Burns, Dracula+).
  - A family line covers only the names it lists, or the explicit "the X themes" set. Examples: Tomorrow, Tokyo Night, LiquidCarbon, CobaltNext, Black Metal, Farmhouse.
  - Unlisted variants drop as "no unambiguous credit": Carbonfox, Dawnfox, Duskfox and Nordfox (the Nightfox line names only Nightfox, Dayfox and Terafox); Poimandres Darker, Storm and White; Gruvbox Material Dark and Light; Claude Dark and Light; Snazzy; Hopscotch.256; the base and Tinted Modus themes.
  - Hand-mapped renames: Blueberry Banana to Banana Blueberry; DjangoSmoothy to Django Smooth; coffee to Coffee Theme; Elementary OS to Elementary; Embers to Embers Dark; PaleNight to Pale Night Hc. All of them drop on other grounds.
- **Mark Badolato's own themes (166) drop.** Their credits have no link or link a terminalthemes.com profile, and the collection LICENSE states that per-theme licensing belongs to each author. If the maintainer confirms in writing that his own themes are MIT under the collection licence, this is the largest single bucket to promote.
- **Forks and copies of the collection drop.** That covers hongzimao, fcsmJS (now mapleDevJS), vitalymak, daytonn, phosphorcube and lemonlime0x3C33 (Sugarplum). Their licence, where present, is the collection's per-theme disclaimer.
- **Port and adapter rule (rule 4 applied consistently).**
  - If the credit links the port's own repository and that repository is licensed, it is resolved.
  - If the person who ported, adapted, converted or based the theme on something has no repository of their own, it is dropped. The origin's licence is recorded in `license_seen`. This includes the mechanical `ditto` conversions (GitHub, GitLab, Horizon, Nord-wave) and Noctis.
- **"Found here" and "taken from" lines.** These use the linked repository as the source. Everforest and Material Ocean link existing iTerm2 colour files, so the contributor who "ported" them is treated as having imported them. Zenbones, Tokyo Night, Nightfox, Tomorrow and Smyck are covered the same way.

**Resolved entries to double-check**
- **Ports where the credited creator's origin has no licence drop.** These are Mirage, Overnight Slumber and the six Karma themes, eight in all.
  - The credit names someone else as creator, and the licensed repository belongs to the porter: `Gomah/mirage-iterm`, `Gomah/overnight-slumber-iterm`, `aspatari/iterm2-karma`.
  - The origins `tristanremy/mirage` and `cevr/overnight` have no licence file; Karma's origin is a website.
  - This mirrors the drops where the origin is licensed but the porter is not. Their porter licence texts are in `licenses-dropped/`.
- **Lineage only.** Atlas Ragnarok, One Dark Two, One Double, Shokunin, Rebecca and Claude stay resolved. In each, the credit names the repository owner as creator, and "based on", "built on" or "inspired by" is only lineage.
- **Nord Light stays resolved.** Its pinned `LICENSE.md` is an MIT grant in the name of the credited creator, Arctic Ice Studio.
- **NOASSERTION read by hand.** GitHub reports NOASSERTION for `chriskempson/tomorrow-theme` and `hukl/Smyck-Color-Scheme`. Both texts are verbatim MIT: one adds a heading line, the other a URL after the copyright line. They are accepted as MIT.
- **Rider scan.** Every accepted MIT text contains the canonical MIT body with nothing after it. The Apache-2.0, ISC, BSD-3-Clause, Zlib and Unlicense texts were read and are unmodified.
- **Renamed repositories.** These are recorded under their canonical `full_name`:
  - `techfitmaster/ghostty-claude-theme` is now `zanehu-ai/ghostty-claude-theme`.
  - `mcchrish/zenbones.nvim` is now `zenbones-theme/zenbones.nvim`.
  - `edeneast/nightfox.nvim` is now `EdenEast/nightfox.nvim`.
- **AI-assisted but resolved.** For theebest-themes and Clockwork Gold the credits say the palettes are human-designed and only the YAML was produced with AI assistance. They are resolved on their repository licences.
- **Copyleft and missing licences.**
  - Kurokula (GPL-3.0) and the Modus colour-blind variants (palettes from GPL-3.0 `protesilaos/modus-themes`) drop. The Kurokula and Sugarplum licence texts are kept for reference in `licenses-dropped/`.
  - Breeze is based on KDE/konsole and drops, with `license_seen` NOASSERTION.
  - `zenorocha/dracula-theme` is a stub repository with no licence. The current Dracula repositories were not substituted.

**Other drops**
- **Dropped with modification by the collection.** PaulMillr (ported "by me") and Seti (port then "slightly modified") drop as collection-side adaptations with no licensed repository of their own.
- **Grok Day and Grok Night.** `promptegrity/grok-themes` has no licence file, so both drop.

**Files**
- `_archive/` holds the pinned theme tarball (sha256 matches `linux/ghostty-resources.env`), used only to confirm that the theme files have no header comments.
- `licenses-dropped/` holds licence texts fetched for repositories that ended up dropped.
- Neither is a deliverable.
- `cache.json` is the per-repository API cache: `full_name`, fork/source, revision, licence path and SPDX.
- `licenses/` holds exactly the files that resolved entries reference.
